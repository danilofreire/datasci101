# Lecture 01: Introduction

This lecture introduces the course and provides a high-level overview of Artificial Intelligence. We discuss the different types of AI, why the field is growing so fast, and some of the main challenges we will study this semester.

## Main Ideas

### 1. Course Logistics

* **Team and Tools**: Introduction to the instructor, TAs, and how we will use GitHub and Canvas.
* **Grading**: Overview of problem sets, in-class quizzes, and the final project.
* **Learning Goals**: Developing a critical eye for AI claims and learning to design realistic AI applications.

### 2. What is AI?

* **Definitions**: A branch of computer science focused on tasks that usually require human intelligence.
* **Types of AI**: The difference between Narrow AI (specific tasks), General AI (human-like), and Superintelligence.
* **AI Taxonomy**: How machine learning, deep learning, and natural language processing fit together.

### 3. Why AI Matters

* **Daily Impacts**: How AI already drives recommendations, healthcare, and finance.
* **The Hype Cycle**: Distinguishing between what AI can actually do and what is just marketing.
* **Core Challenges**: Introduction to bias, hallucinations, and why AI systems sometimes fail.
