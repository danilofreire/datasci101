# Lecture 22: AI and Wellbeing: The Attention Economy, Mental Health, and the Environment

This lecture explores how AI affects our wellbeing in three areas: the attention economy that shapes what we see online, the promise and risks of AI in mental healthcare, and the environmental costs of training and running large models.

## Topics Covered

- **The Attention Economy**: How recommendation algorithms work, filter bubbles, engagement vs wellbeing, and what the data says about teens and screens
- **AI and Mental Healthcare**: The treatment gap, AI as a therapeutic tool, what the research shows, and what LLMs cannot do
- **AI and the Environment**: The energy and water costs of training and inference, the footprint in context, and AI as a climate tool
- **Design Responses**: What companies are doing, China's algorithm regulations, and designing for wellbeing

## Learning Objectives

After this lecture, students should be able to:
1. Explain how recommendation systems optimise for engagement and why that can conflict with wellbeing
2. Evaluate the evidence linking social media and digital technology to mental health outcomes
3. Assess the benefits and risks of AI tools in mental healthcare
4. Describe the environmental costs of training and running AI models
